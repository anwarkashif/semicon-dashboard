Risk and Threat Analysis:
The semiconductor industry is transitioning from a model of hyper-globalization to one of strategic autonomy. The primary risk remains the weaponization of critical mineral supply chains and the potential for retaliatory trade measures that could disrupt the flow of legacy and advanced chips. Regionalization efforts are creating redundant capacity but are simultaneously driving up operational costs and creating long-term talent acquisition challenges.

Predictive Analysis:
Over the next 18 months, expect a surge in 'friend-shoring' agreements as nations seek to secure upstream mineral supplies. We anticipate further tightening of dual-use technology export restrictions, likely leading to a bifurcated global standard for semiconductor architecture and manufacturing processes.
